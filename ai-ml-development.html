AI Engineering FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions: Enterprise AI

Essential technical insights on data confidentiality, sovereign deployments, accuracy evaluation, and production SLAs.

How does DecillionX ensure enterprise data privacy with LLM deployments?
DecillionX implements zero-data-leakage architecture. Client training data and corporate documents are processed within dedicated, isolated virtual private clouds (VPC) or sovereign on-premise hardware. Data is never shared with public model providers, and all vector databases are encrypted with AES-256 keys managed by the client.
Can DecillionX deploy open-source models on private on-premise or sovereign cloud infrastructure?
Yes. We specialize in quantizing and deploying state-of-the-art open models (Llama 3, Mistral, DeepSeek, Gemma) using vLLM, TensorRT-LLM, and Ollama directly on private AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, or bare-metal Kubernetes GPU clusters with complete data sovereignty.
How does DecillionX benchmark and evaluate Generative AI accuracy and hallucination reduction?
We deploy automated continuous evaluation frameworks using RAGAS, TruLens, and G-Eval to quantify context recall, precision, and faithfulness. Hallucination guardrails and semantic validation filters intercept and verify responses before they reach end users.
What is the typical timeframe to deliver an enterprise AI proof-of-concept (POC)?
Our AI squads deliver an interactive, benchmarked functional proof-of-concept (POC) connected to your enterprise data within 2 to 3 weeks, followed by iterative enterprise hardening and production deployment.
