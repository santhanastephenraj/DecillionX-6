Location & Engagement FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about DecillionX corporate offices, global delivery centers, engagement models, and meeting scheduling.

Where is DecillionX located in the United States?
DecillionX's North American Operations are located in Plano, Texas 75025, United States. From this hub, we provide executive enterprise consulting, solution architecture reviews, and client partnership management for Fortune 500 enterprises and mid-market organizations across North America.
Where is DecillionX located in India?
DecillionX's Global Engineering Delivery Headquarters are located in Indiranagar, Bangalore 560038, Karnataka, India. Bangalore houses our dedicated full-stack engineering squads, AI/LLM research labs, and cloud DevOps infrastructure centers.
What services are provided across the Plano and Bangalore offices?
Both locations collaborate to deliver custom enterprise software development, ERP and CRM engineering, Generative AI and autonomous LLM agents, cloud infrastructure across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud, Kubernetes DevOps, cybersecurity VAPT auditing, and dedicated engineering squad augmentation.
How does DecillionX's dual-location model benefit global enterprise clients?
Our dual footprint spans US Central Time (CT) and Indian Standard Time (IST), creating a continuous 24/7 follow-the-sun development cycle. Clients receive live executive collaboration during US hours and rapid overnight development execution from India, cutting time-to-market by up to 60%.
Can we schedule in-person meetings at DecillionX offices?
Yes. Executive discovery sessions, technical architecture reviews, and project kickoffs can be scheduled by appointment at our Plano, Texas 75025 office or our Indiranagar, Bangalore 560038 headquarters. Email info@decillionx.com to coordinate your session.
Does DecillionX execute mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)?
Yes. Prior to receiving any proprietary documentation, project specifications, or database schemas, DecillionX executes a standard mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (M-NDA) governed by United States law, ensuring 100% intellectual property protection and confidentiality.
