Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

DecillionX Software & AI Development (Offices in Plano, Texas 75025, USA and Indiranagar, Bangalore 560038, India) is committed to stringent data privacy, GDPR, and DPDP India compliance.

1. Information Collection & Usage

DecillionX collects client business information, legal verification details (GSTIN/CIN), and candidate talent profiles solely for authentic enterprise project engagement and employment screening. Information is encrypted at rest and in transit using SSL/TLS 256-bit encryption.

2. Cookie Policy & Preferences

We use essential session cookies to authenticate client portal and employee dashboard sessions. We do not sell user data to third-party ad networks. You may manage cookie consent via your browser settings.

In accordance with India DPDP Act 2023 and EU GDPR regulations, clients and job applicants have the right to request access, correction, or permanent deletion of their personal data from our database. Requests can be sent directly to CEO Santhana Stephen Raj at info@decillionx.com.
