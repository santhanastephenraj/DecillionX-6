Cybersecurity FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions: Cybersecurity & VAPT

Clear answers on penetration testing scope, remediation support, certifications, and emergency timelines.

What is the scope of a standard DecillionX VAPT penetration testing audit?
A standard VAPT audit encompasses black-box, gray-box, and white-box penetration testing covering web portals, iOS/Android mobile apps, external API endpoints, internal network perimeters, cloud IAM roles, and OWASP Top 10 vulnerabilities with proof-of-concept exploits.
How does DecillionX help enterprises remediate identified vulnerabilities?
Rather than handing over an unassisted PDF report, our senior security engineers conduct live debriefs with your development team, provide precise code-level patch snippets, and perform free re-testing validation to certify complete remediation.
Are DecillionX security engineers certified by international cybersecurity bodies?
Yes. Our offensive security engineers hold premier industry certifications including OSCP (Offensive Security Certified Professional), CEH (Certified Ethical Hacker), CISSP, and AWS/Azure Certified Security Specialists.
How quickly can DecillionX begin an urgent emergency security incident audit?
Our incident response and penetration testing teams can initiate an emergency reconnaissance audit within 24 to 48 hours of executed mutual NDA and rules-of-engagement authorization.
