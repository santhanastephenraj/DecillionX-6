Client Engagement FAQ

Working With DecillionX: Proposals & SLAs

Everything you need to know about starting an engagement, scheduling architect meetings, and our mutual NDAs.

How can I request a formal proposal or technical RFP consultation with DecillionX?
You can submit an RFP through our contact form, email us directly at info@decillionx.com, or use the Get Quote button on our website. Our principal software architects analyze your requirements and deliver a customized technical proposal and budget estimate within 24 business hours.
What is DecillionX's guaranteed response time for enterprise support inquiries?
For general inquiries, our team responds within 24 hours. For enterprise clients under active SLA maintenance agreements, our 24/7 technical incident response team provides critical incident acknowledgement within 15 minutes.
Can we schedule in-person meetings at DecillionX offices?
Yes. Executive client discovery sessions and technical architecture reviews can be scheduled by appointment at our North American Operations office in Plano, Texas 75025, or our Global Engineering Headquarters in Indiranagar, Bangalore 560038.
Does DecillionX execute mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) prior to project discovery?
Yes. Prior to receiving any proprietary documentation, project specifications, or database schemas, DecillionX executes a standard mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (M-NDA) protecting all trade secrets and intellectual property.
