About DecillionX: Leadership & Operations

Essential information about our corporate leadership, global footprint, delivery standards, and client partnerships.

Who founded DecillionX and what is the company's executive leadership structure?
DecillionX was founded and is led by CEO Santhana Stephen Raj. Our leadership team brings decades of combined engineering experience in enterprise ERP architecture, generative AI systems, high-scale cloud platforms, and cybersecurity governance.
Where are DecillionX global headquarters and engineering delivery centers located?
DecillionX operates two primary corporate locations: North American Operations headquartered in Plano, Texas 75025, United States, and Global Engineering & AI Delivery Headquarters located in Indiranagar, Bangalore 560038, Karnataka, India.
