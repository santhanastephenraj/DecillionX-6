Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Everything you need to know about partnering with DecillionX for custom software engineering, generative AI deployment, and enterprise technology consulting.

What is DecillionX and what services does it offer?
DecillionX is a premier global enterprise software and Artificial Intelligence corporation. We specialize in custom enterprise software & ERP engineering, generative AI & autonomous LLM agents, cloud-native DevOps & Kubernetes, cybersecurity VAPT audits, web/mobile applications, and strategic IT consulting for Fortune 500s and high-growth enterprises.
Where is DecillionX located in the United States and India?
DecillionX operates with dual global presence: North American Operations headquartered in Plano, Texas 75025, United States, and Global Engineering Delivery & AI Headquarters located in Indiranagar, Bangalore 560038, Karnataka, India.
Does DecillionX provide custom software development and ERP engineering?
Yes. DecillionX engineers custom enterprise software, multi-tenant SaaS platforms, distributed microservices, and bespoke ERP/CRM architectures built on modern tech stacks including Python, Node.js, Go, React, and Kubernetes.
Does DecillionX provide AI/ML, Generative AI, and Autonomous LLM services?
Yes. We engineer private, secure generative AI pipelines, custom fine-tuned LLMs, autonomous multi-agent systems, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and computer vision models with enterprise zero-data-leakage architecture.
Does DecillionX provide cloud consulting, Kubernetes, and DevOps services?
Yes. We architect resilient multi-cloud environments across AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure, implementing GitOps CI/CD pipelines, Kubernetes container orchestration (EKS/GKE/AKS), Terraform IaC, and 24/7 site reliability engineering.
How does DecillionX compare to traditional IT outsourcing and legacy software companies?
Unlike traditional IT outsourcing firms that rely on junior developer rotation, DecillionX deploys dedicated senior AI and software squads, guarantees 100% client IP ownership from day one under US law, executes 24/7 follow-the-sun sprints, and provides strict 99.99% availability SLAs.
How does DecillionX handle enterprise IP, cybersecurity, and data compliance?
All code and models developed for clients are 100% client-owned proprietary intellectual property under strict NDA. Our architectures follow ISO 27001 Information Security, SOC 2 Type II, EU GDPR, and India DPDP Act 2023 compliance with zero-trust networking and data encryption in transit and at rest.
How can organizations request an executive proposal or schedule an architecture review?
You can request an executive corporate proposal via our online proposal RFP form or by emailing info@decillionx.com. Our principal software architects respond within 24 hours with scoping, architecture roadmaps, and sprint timelines.
