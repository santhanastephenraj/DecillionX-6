IT Consulting FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions: IT Strategy

Clear answers on consulting scope, fractional CTO engagement, ROI calculation, and audit durations.

What is the scope of DecillionX executive IT consulting engagements?
Our IT consulting engagements span enterprise technology audits, technical debt mitigation roadmaps, cloud modernization blueprints, AI readiness assessments, vendor evaluations, and CTO advisory for boards and executive leadership teams.
Can DecillionX provide fractional or interim CTO advisory for scaling enterprises?
Yes. DecillionX principal architects and CEO Santhana Stephen Raj serve as trusted fractional CTOs and technical advisors for enterprises navigating major technology migrations, fundraising technical audits, or high-concurrency re-architectures.
How does DecillionX calculate ROI on digital transformation initiatives?
We establish clear baseline KPIs before writing code: infrastructure expenditure reductions, developer velocity acceleration, mean time to recovery (MTTR) improvements, and automated manual operational labor cost savings.
How quickly can DecillionX conduct an enterprise technology architecture audit?
A standard rapid enterprise architecture audit is completed within 10 to 14 business days, yielding a prioritized architectural gap report, risk assessment matrix, and pragmatic multi-phase execution roadmap.
